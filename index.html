        Learning path
        
From zero to professional

        
Follow the stages in order. Each one builds on the one before.

      

      

        
  1. 1
    Stage 1
    Meet the terminal
    Run commands, read their output, find out where you are.
    Open the lesson: Your first steps in the Linux terminal 
  2. 2
    Stage 2
    Files and folders
    Create, copy, move and delete without a mouse.
    Open the lesson: Files and folders on the command line 
  3. 3
    Stage 3
    Permissions and users
    Who may read, change or run each file.
    Open the lesson: Understanding file permissions 
  4. 4
    Stage 4
    Text tools and pipes
    grep, sort, wc and joining commands with | to answer questions from logs.
    Coming soon
  5. 5
    Stage 5
    Automation with scripts
    Turn the commands you repeat into one script.
    Open the lesson: Automate with Bash scripts 
  6. 6
    Stage 6
    Running a server
    Packages, services (systemctl), SSH, logs and backups — the daily work of an administrator.
    Coming soon

        
How to study here
  1. Read one lesson slowly, start to finish.
  2. Type every command yourself in the practice lab — don't just read it.
  3. Do the exercises at the end before you open the answers.
  4. Ask the guide for a quiz on the topic the next day.
  5. Finish each stage by building something small of your own.
