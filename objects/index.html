Object detection with voice

Point your camera at things around you: it finds 80 kinds of everyday objects and can say their names out loud.

Everything runs on your device. Your camera image never leaves your phone or computer.

Start the camera or upload a photo Try a cup, a phone, a laptop, a chair or a book.

—

How it works

One look per frame

EfficientDet-Lite0, a small neural network, looks at each frame and draws a box around every object it recognises, with how sure it is.

80 everyday objects

People, cups, bottles, phones, laptops, keyboards, chairs, books, fruit, animals, vehicles and more — the COCO set.

Speech on your device

Names are read with your device's built-in voice. New objects are announced once, so it doesn't repeat itself.

Built with Google's open-source MediaPipe and EfficientDet-Lite0 (Apache 2.0), hosted on this site.
